REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Solicitation Number: W31P4Q-27-R-DEMO
Issuing Office: U.S. Army Contracting Command – Redstone Arsenal, AL
On behalf of: Program Executive Office Missiles and Space (PEO MS), Lower Tier Project Office
Program: Integrated Air and Missile Defense (IAMD) Interceptor Production Ramp & Digital Sustainment (IPR-DS)
NAICS Code: 336414 – Guided Missile and Space Vehicle Manufacturing
Set-Aside: None – Full and Open Competition
Contract Type: Hybrid Firm-Fixed-Price (production) / Cost-Plus-Incentive-Fee (engineering & sustainment)
Period of Performance: One (1) 12-month base period plus four (4) 12-month option periods

1. BACKGROUND
Demand for hit-to-kill interceptors has grown sharply due to operational expenditures and
allied Foreign Military Sales. The Government seeks a contractor to increase interceptor
production capacity, modernize the production and sustainment enterprise using digital
engineering, and improve fleet readiness for fielded Patriot-class interceptor inventories.

2. SCOPE OF WORK
2.1 Production Ramp
  a. Increase interceptor production from current rates to at least 650 units per year by the
     end of Option Period 2.
  b. Qualify second sources for long-lead components, including seekers, rocket motors, and
     energetics, and deliver a Supply Chain Resilience Plan.
  c. Maintain First Article Test and Lot Acceptance Test compliance throughout the ramp.
2.2 Digital Engineering & MOSA
  a. Establish an authoritative digital thread linking design, production, test, and sustainment data.
  b. Deliver a digital twin of the interceptor production line to model throughput and bottlenecks.
  c. Apply a Modular Open Systems Approach (MOSA) to seeker and guidance electronics to enable
     future technology insertion.
2.3 Sustainment & Readiness
  a. Provide performance-based logistics for fielded interceptors, with a readiness target of 90%.
  b. Implement predictive maintenance and stockpile reliability analytics.
  c. Support recertification of aging inventory.
2.4 Cybersecurity
  a. Achieve and maintain CMMC Level 2 across the prime and critical suppliers.
  b. Support Risk Management Framework (RMF) activities for production test equipment and
     connected factory systems.
2.5 Test & Evaluation
  a. Support developmental and operational flight tests, including test planning, range
     coordination, and post-test data analysis.

3. DELIVERABLES
  - Production Ramp Plan (30 days after award)
  - Supply Chain Resilience Plan (60 days after award)
  - Digital Thread Architecture Document and Production Digital Twin (180 days after award)
  - MOSA Interface Control Documents
  - Monthly Program Management Reviews and Contract Performance Reports (EVM)
  - Readiness and Reliability Reports (quarterly)
  - Cybersecurity / CMMC Compliance Reports (semi-annual)
  - Flight Test Reports

4. EVALUATION CRITERIA (Best Value Tradeoff)
  Factor 1 – Technical Approach (Production Ramp & Digital Engineering) – most important
  Factor 2 – Management Approach & Supply Chain Risk
  Factor 3 – Past Performance (relevant interceptor or missile production programs)
  Factor 4 – Small Business Participation Plan (target: 25% of subcontract dollars)
  Factor 5 – Cost/Price
  Factors 1 through 4, combined, are significantly more important than cost/price.

5. SUBMISSION
  Proposals are due 45 days after release. Volume page limits: Technical 50, Management 30,
  Past Performance 15. Questions must be submitted through the Procurement Integrated
  Enterprise Environment (PIEE) within 15 days of release.
